About Furry Temple

Play together.Build your world.

Furry Temple is an adult (18+) online sandbox where you play as your own furry character. Join public rooms or invite friends to roleplay, chat, explore maps and play games created by the community. Each map can have its own activities, interactive objects and rules.

Customize your character’s body proportions and appearance, then paint every part directly on the 3D model. Brushes, layers and symmetry tools let you create detailed patterns and control your colors. Save different looks, publish them in the character library and discover designs made by other players.

Build and edit maps with friends: sculpt terrain, arrange objects, create your own furniture and interactive props, and share them with other players. Set the lighting and weather, add animations and interfaces, and use Blueprint to turn your ideas into working game mechanics without writing code.

Game features

Character creation and painting

Shape your character’s proportions and appearance, then paint the whole model or focus on one part for detail work. Combine brushes, layers and symmetry to create sharp patterns, soft color transitions and fine details. Create skins from scratch or edit existing ones, and share your skins with other players.

Multiplayer and roleplay

Meet other players in public rooms or gather with friends in a private session. Explore maps, roleplay and play together. Add friends and exchange messages. Built-in AI text translation helps players who speak different languages understand each other.

Build worlds together

Work on the same map with friends, from the landscape to the furnished interior. Sculpt hills, smooth paths, construct buildings and arrange objects. Adjust the time of day, indoor and outdoor lighting, clouds, rain and thunderstorms to set the atmosphere of your world.

Create and share objects

Make your own furniture, decorations and interactive props with built-in modeling tools. Combine and cut shapes, edit geometry and apply materials. Save your creation as a reusable object with its behavior, publish it in the community library, or bring other players’ creations into your map.

Blueprint game logic

Connect events, conditions and actions to define how your game works. Use variables and reusable functions to track scores, control NPCs and create objectives, interactions and game rules. Connect objects, physics and interfaces into complete game systems through a visual graph, without writing code.

Interfaces and animation

Design game menus, interactive screens and status panels in the 2D editor, then connect their controls and data to Blueprint. Animate objects and create character poses and animation sequences with a keyframe timeline. Add sounds and particle effects that respond to events in your game.

FAQ

More about the game

What is Furry Temple?

Furry Temple is an adult (18+) online furry sandbox for social play, roleplay and creating your own content. Join a room to play on its map with other people, or build your own world and decide what players can do there. Character painting, map editing and visual game creation are built into the game.

How can I customize my character?

Adjust body proportions and appearance, then paint all parts of your character directly in 3D. Use different brushes, layers, symmetry and color groups to build and refine your design. Create skins from scratch or edit existing ones, share your work and discover skins made by other players.

Can I build a map with friends?

Yes. Invite friends into the same editing session and work on the map together. One person can shape the landscape while another builds a house, furnishes a room or sets up an interactive object. The shared editor brings terrain, objects, lighting and game logic into one project.

Can I make my own furniture and objects?

Yes. Use the built-in modeling tools to combine shapes, cut openings, edit geometry and add materials. Turn your work into reusable furniture, scenery or interactive props, with Blueprint behavior attached. Publish objects in the community library and use shared creations in your own maps.

How do I create game mechanics?

Blueprint connects events, conditions and actions in a visual graph. Variables and functions let you track game state and reuse logic across objects. Create scoring systems, interactive puzzles, NPC behavior and objectives, then connect them to your own interface without writing code.

Can I create interfaces and animations?

The 2D editor lets you build menus, interactive screens and status displays with buttons, text, images and other controls, then link them to Blueprint. Animation tools provide a timeline and keyframes for objects and characters, so you can create moving props, poses and animation sequences for your map.

What does AI translation support?

The built-in AI translator helps players who speak different languages communicate in text chat. Choose manual translation when you need it or automatic translation for ongoing conversations, and switch between the translation and the original message.

How do I play with friends?

Choose a map and create a room for your group, or join an existing public room together. Add friends and use in-game messages to stay in touch while you play. You can also explore built-in solo maps on your own.

Is this an adult game?

Yes. Furry Temple is an adult game intended for players aged 18 and over.
